## Who can help run a game?

Every game has one **owner**, the person who created it. The owner can invite two kinds of helper:

- **Co-hosts** run the event like the owner: buzzer, scores, teams, settings, and inviting other helpers.
- **Moderators** help with players, such as moving someone to the right team. They don't run the buzzer or change scores.

Only the owner can delete the game. Nobody can remove the owner or change the owner's role. See Rules at a glance for exactly what each role can do.

## How do I invite a helper?

1. Open the host desk and go to the **Room** tab. Helpers are listed under **Helpers**.
2. Choose **Co-host** or **Moderator**.
3. Select **Create invitation link**, then **Copy link**, and send it to your helper privately, for example in a direct message.

Each link works once and expires after a day. Anyone who has the link can accept it until then, so don't post it in a public chat. To cancel an unused link, select **Cancel invitation** under **Unused invitations**.

## How do I accept an invitation?

Open the link. It shows the game and the role you're offered. Sign in if you're asked to, then select **Accept**. You go straight to the host desk.

Helpers always sign in with an account; a player's game code alone never gives helper access. You don't need to join as a player first.

## Why does my invitation say it can't be used?

- **Already been used:** someone has accepted it. Each link works for one person.
- **Expired** or **cancelled:** ask the host to create a new link.

If you accepted a link and were later removed, opening the same link again won't restore your role. Ask the host for a new one.

## What if I'm already a helper?

Accepting an invitation never lowers your role. If you're a co-host and open a moderator link, you stay a co-host. A moderator who accepts a co-host link becomes a co-host.

## How do I change or remove a helper?

In the **Room** tab, under **Helpers**, use the role menu next to a helper to switch them between **Co-host** and **Moderator**, or select **Remove**. The change takes effect immediately: a removed helper can't use the host desk any more, and their live view stops updating. Removing a helper doesn't sign them out of other games.
