Buzz-Board has two ways to buzz. Choose one when you create a game, and switch between questions from the **Buzzing** menu on the **Play** tab. Whichever you use, **Undo** and **Redo** fix accidental presses.

## What's the difference between first buzz and queue?

| | **First buzz wins** | **Queue** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Who answers | Only the fastest buzz. The buzzer closes at once. | Everyone who buzzes lines up in order. The first in line answers. |
| A wrong answer | Select **Wrong: let others buzz** to reopen for everyone else. | Select **Wrong** and the next team in line answers straight away. |
| Best for | Quick-fire rounds and small groups. | Bigger groups, families on a call, and many teams, so nobody's buzz is lost. |

In both modes, a team that answered wrongly sits out the rest of that question. **Next question** lets everyone buzz again.

## How does the queue work?

1. Select **Open buzzer**. Each team can be in the queue once. Players see their place, for example "You're next in line".
2. The first team in line is **Answering**. Mark them:
   - **Correct** closes the buzzer. Award points with **Award N to Team**.
   - **Wrong** locks that team out for this question, and the next team answers.
   - **Skip** moves on without locking the team out (for example, they couldn't hear). They can buzz again.
3. **Remove** takes a waiting team out of the line, for example after an accidental press.
4. **Close buzzer** stops new buzzes while you work through the line. **Reopen buzzer** lets late buzzes join the back of the same queue.
5. Select **Next question** when you're done. After a correct answer, **Open buzzer for a new question** does both at once.

The audience display shows the queue in large type, with who's answering highlighted.

## Can I change the buzzing mode during a game?

Yes, between questions. Close the buzzer first: the **Buzzing** menu is locked while the buzzer is open. Changing mode starts a fresh question.

## How do Undo and Redo work?

Every buzzer action (open, close, next question, correct, wrong, skip, remove, and changing mode) can be undone. The **Undo** button names the action it will reverse, for example **Undo: Next question**, so you know what you're undoing. **Redo** puts it back.

- Undo brings back the buzzer exactly as it was: open or closed, who was answering, who was locked out, and the whole queue.
- A buzz that arrives after the action you undo is kept, and stays in line behind the teams that were already there.
- Doing something new, or a player buzzing, clears **Redo**.
- **Recent buzzer actions** lists what happened, with times.

Scores have their own **Undo score** and **Redo** in the **Scores** panel, so undoing a buzzer mistake never changes anyone's points.
