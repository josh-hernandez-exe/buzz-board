## What is the audience display?

It's a read-only scoreboard for the people watching. It shows the game's name, the join code while joining is open, each team's score in large type, who buzzed in (or the whole queue, in queue mode), and a ★ next to the leader. It has no controls, so it's safe to screen-share in a meeting. Open it from the host desk with **Open audience display**.

Anyone with the display's link can see it without signing in, so share the link only where the scores can be public.

## Why is there no sound on the audience display?

Sound starts off so that every open copy of the display doesn't play at once. On the one computer that shares its audio with your meeting, select **Turn on sound**. You'll then hear a chime when someone buzzes in and a buzz when a team is ruled out.

## How do I change scores?

On the host desk's **Play** tab:

- **Award N to Team** in the **Buzzer** panel gives points to whoever holds the buzz, in one tap.
- In the **Scores** panel, set **Points per tap**, then use **+** or **−** next to any team.
- For several teams at once, or to set an exact score, open **More score tools**. Tick the teams (or use **Buzzer Selected** or **Buzzer Rejected** to pick them), type a whole number, and choose **Add**, **Subtract** or **Set**. **Set** replaces the score, so **Set** with 0 resets it.

Each click is saved once, even if your connection retries it. Scores have limits (see Rules at a glance).

## How do undo and redo work?

**Undo** steps back through score changes one at a time, and **Redo** steps forward again in the same order. Making a new change after undoing clears the changes you could have redone. While a game mode (Ranked Answers or Category Grid) is running, **Undo score** only reaches changes made since the mode last changed the scores, so the mode's board and the scores stay in step. The mode has its own **Undo last action**.

**Undo score** and **Redo** are only for scores: they don't change the buzzer or teams. The buzzer has its own undo; see [Buzzing modes and undo](/help/buzzing).

## Can two hosts change scores at the same time?

Yes. Changes are applied one after another, so none are lost. If the game is very busy, a change may be refused with "The game is busy". Wait a moment and try again: your change was not saved.

## What does "Reconnecting… scores and buzzers may be out of date" mean?

The page lost its live connection and is trying to reconnect. Your buzzer still works, but the scores you see may be behind. If the message changes to "Live updates stopped", reload the page.
