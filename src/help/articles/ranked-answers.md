Ranked Answers is a family-feud style game mode. Each board has a prompt, like "Things people hang on a holiday tree", and 4 to 8 hidden answers ranked from most to least popular. Teams guess them, and every answer found is revealed on everyone's screen with its points. Points go straight onto the normal scoreboard.

## How do I set up boards?

You can choose Ranked Answers when you create a game: pick **Ranked Answers (family-feud style)** under **Game**, choose how boards are played, and optionally start with sample boards. The host desk then opens on the **Ranked Answers** tab. You can also set it up later in any game: owners and co-hosts open the **Ranked Answers** tab on the host desk.

1. Choose **how boards are played** (see below). Open **Fine-tune the rules** to change any rule, and the **points by rank**. The default is 30, 25, 20, 15, 10, 8, 5, 2 for answers 1 to 8.
2. **Add board** for each prompt. List the answers from most to least popular. **Also accept** lists other wordings you'll count as right ("baubles" for "ornaments").
3. To use your own numbers, such as how many people gave each answer in a survey you ran, tick **Set points for each answer**. Each answer can't be worth more than the one above it.
4. Mark a board **Practice** to warm up without points, or **Spare** to keep it for a tie-break. Give any board a **multiplier**, for example 2× for a final round.
5. **Save boards.** Anything under **Fix before starting** must be fixed first; **Worth knowing** is just advice.
6. When everyone has joined, **Start Ranked Answers**. The boards are locked from then on.

Not sure where to begin? **Load sample boards** fills in a practice board, three holiday boards and a spare that you can edit. **Import or export** shows your boards as text you can keep as a backup or paste into another game.

Keep your answers private: they're only shown to owners and co-hosts until revealed.

## Which rules should I choose?

Pick a starting point. Each is just a set of rules, so you can change any of them afterwards. Every style is made for groups, so there's no two-team-only format: the TV show's rules live on as **Classic feud**, for 3 to 8 teams.

| Starting point | Teams | How it works |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Open answering** | 2 to 50 | Trivia style, with no turns: any team can call out an answer at any time. You reveal each answer and tap every team that got it; each scores it. Suggested for 3 or more teams. |
| **Classic feud** | 3 to 8 | The TV format, for a crowd. One player from each of two teams faces off; the pair changes every board so every team gets its turns. The winner chooses to play or pass. The playing team keeps answering until it finds every answer or gets 3 strikes; then **every other team** gives one steal guess at once. The best steal takes all the points found on that board (the bank); other correct steals score their own answer. |
| **Classic feud, head-to-head** | 3 to 8 | Like Classic feud, but only the face-off opponent can steal: each board is a two-team match while the others watch. |
| **Take turns, no face-off** | 3 to 8 | Like Classic feud, but the teams take turns playing whole boards in rotation, with no face-off. |
| **Round robin** | 2 to 12 | Teams answer one at a time, and the starting team changes each board. Each answer scores for the team that found it. |
| **Pass-along** | 2 to 12 | A team keeps answering until it misses; then the next team takes over. Each answer scores for the team that found it. |
| **Buzz race** | 2 to 50 | Every answer is a race: teams buzz, and the first answers. A right answer scores for that team. A wrong one sits the team out until the next answer is found. Works best with queued buzzing. |
| **All-play** | 2 to 50 | Every team answers at the same time, in a few rounds per board. Every team that finds an answer scores it. Rounds take the same time whatever the number of teams. |
| **All-play, unique answers only** | 2 to 50 | Like All-play, but an answer only scores if exactly one team found it. Rewards thinking differently. |

## What can I change in the rules?

Under **Fine-tune the rules**, only the rules that matter for your style are shown:

| Rule | Choices |
| --- | --- |
| **Face-off** | **Buzzer**: the first to buzz answers first, which suits a room where everyone can hear. **Secret**: both answer privately and the higher answer wins, which is fairer online because connection speed can't decide it. **None**: teams take turns playing boards. |
| **Face-off winner** | Chooses to play or pass, or always plays. |
| **Points while playing** | **Bank**: points build up and go to whoever wins the board. **Direct**: every answer scores straight away for the team that found it. |
| **Strikes** | 1 to 5 wrong answers before the playing team's turn ends. |
| **Steal by** | The face-off opponent, every other team at once, or nobody (the board just ends). |
| **A successful steal wins** | The bank plus the answer, or just the answer (the playing team keeps the bank). |
| **Several correct steals** | The highest answer takes the bank, or every correct team does. |
| **Answers or turns per team** | Round robin and Pass-along: how many times each team gets a go per board. |
| **Rounds per board** | All-play: how many rounds before the board closes. |
| **Same answer found by several teams** | All-play and Open answering: each gets the full points, they split them, or nobody scores it. |
| **After a wrong answer** | Buzz race: sit out until the next answer is found, sit out the rest of the board after a number of misses, or no lockout. If every team is sitting out, everyone gets another go. |
| **Points lost for a miss** | Taken off the team that missed, when it alone was answering. 0 means no penalty. |
| **Target score** | Shown on every screen as "First to 300 points wins", and announced when a team gets there. You still decide when to finish. |
| **Points by rank** and **Points multiplier** | What each answer is worth, and a multiplier for every board. |

## Running a board

Choose **Show this board** to put the next board on every screen. Then follow the buttons on the host desk; they change with each step:

- **Face-off:** pick who buzzed first (Buzzer style) and what each team said, then **Record face-off**. If nobody finds an answer, record it and run the face-off again.
- **Playing:** press the answer the team gave, or **Strike**.
- **Steal:** pick each stealing team's answer, then **Record steal**.
- **Round robin:** press the answer, or **Wrong / pass**, for the team whose turn it is.
- **Open answering:** tap every team that got it, then the answer. Tapping an answer with no team chosen just reveals it for no points. With a miss penalty, choose one team and press **Wrong** to take points off.
- **Pass-along:** press each answer the team gives; **Miss: next team** passes the turn on.
- **Buzz race:** the team holding the buzz is chosen for you (or choose who answered), then press their answer or **Wrong**. With **Also update the buzzer** ticked, the buzzer is marked too: a right answer closes it; a wrong one lets the next team answer, and only locks the team out if your rules sit it out. If every team is sitting out, the buzzer starts afresh so everyone can go again. **Open buzzer** starts the race for the next answer.
- **All-play:** pick every team's answer, then **Reveal round**. Teams can tell you their answer out loud or post it in their team chat.

You judge the answers: the host desk shows each hidden answer and its **also accept** wordings in italics. **Close board** ends a board early, for example for time. After a board, **Reveal remaining answers** shows the rest for 0 points.

Players see the board above their buzzer, and the audience display shows it in large print. Neither ever shows an answer before it's revealed.

## How do I fix a mistake?

**Undo last action** takes back the last step and the points it gave, in one go. An answer that was already shown stays on the board, marked as shown for 0 points, because everyone has seen it. You can undo several steps in a row.

**Void this board** is for a board that went wrong, for example a mistake in its answers. It takes back every point the board gave or took (including miss penalties) and closes it. Answers already shown stay shown, and the board can't be played again, so play a spare board instead. Undo can reverse a void.

Two hosts can run a board together. If you both press something at the same moment, only the first counts, and the other screen updates so you can check before trying again.

You can also change scores by hand on the **Play** tab at any time. The scoreboard's **Undo score** only reaches changes made since Ranked Answers last changed the scores, so it can never take points away from the board. Use **Undo last action** for those.

## How do I go back to the buzzer?

**Use basic controls** stops Ranked Answers for good in this room. Players, teams, chat and scores all stay, and you carry on with the buzzer and manual scores.

- **The buzzer closes.** Open it when you're ready; nothing reopens by itself.
- **Players and the audience display** are told the game carries on with the buzzer.
- **The scoreboard's Undo score** only reaches changes you make after this point.

To play Ranked Answers again, duplicate the game.
