You don't need an account to play. All you need is the game code from your host, or the link they shared.

## How do I join a game?

1. Open the link your host shared, or go to **Join a game** from the home page and type the six-character code.
2. Choose a nickname. Everyone in the game will see it, so pick something you're happy to share.
3. Select **Join game**.

You'll go straight to your buzzer. In a team game, the page shows which team you're on.

## Do I need to sign in?

No. Players can always join as a guest. Hosts sign in so their games are kept safely under their account.

## How do I get back in after closing the page?

Open the same link or code again on the same browser. Buzz-Board remembers you and offers **Resume as** your nickname. You keep your name, team and points.

If several people share one device, choose **Join as someone new** to join as a different player.

## What does "Leave this room on this device" do?

It makes this browser forget you in that game. Your name, team and points stay in the game for the host's records. Use it on a shared or borrowed device.

After leaving, this browser can't resume as you. Joining again creates a new player.

## I lost my device or cleared my browser. Can I get my player back?

Not yet. Join again as a new player and tell your host. They can move you to the right team. Your old points stay with your old player on the scoreboard.

## Why can't I join?

The page tells you why:

- **No game has that code.** Check the code with your host. Codes use letters and numbers, but never the letter O or the number zero.
- **Joining is closed for this game.** The host has stopped new players joining. Ask them to open it. Players already in the game can still come back.
- **This game has finished.** The game is over and can't be joined.

See [Troubleshooting](/help/troubleshooting) for more.
