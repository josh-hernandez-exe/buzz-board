Owners, co-hosts and moderators manage players from the host desk's **Players** tab. Use **Find a player** to search by name or player number. Players with the same name are told apart by their number and team.

## How do I change an inappropriate name?

Select **Rename** next to the player or team, type a new name, and tick **Stop this player renaming themselves** (or **Stop team members renaming it**) so the name stays changed. Both happen in one save. Everyone sees the new name straight away, and points don't change.

A player who is editing their name while you rename them gets a message that it changed, so they can't overwrite your fix. To let them choose a name again, open **Rename** and untick the lock.

You can still correct names after the game has finished.

## How do I move a player to another team?

Choose a team from the menu next to the player, or **No team**. Moves wait while the buzzer is open, so pause it first. Points stay with the team that earned them.

## How do I remove a player?

Select **Remove**, then **Remove player**. It works at once, even while the buzzer is open: the player is signed out of the game and can't come back as that player. A buzz they already won and their team's points stay.

Removing someone doesn't stop them joining again as a new player. Turn off **New players can join** to prevent that.

## A guest lost their device. How do they get back in?

If the player saved themselves to an account, they sign in and choose **Resume as** their name.

Otherwise, an owner or co-host can help:

1. Check it's really them, for example by asking in your meeting.
2. Select **Recovery link** next to their name.
3. Send them the link privately. It works once, for a short time (see Rules at a glance).

Opening the link brings back the same player, with their name, team and points. Their old device is signed out. If the link doesn't work, create a new one; only the newest link works. Never post a recovery link in a public chat.
