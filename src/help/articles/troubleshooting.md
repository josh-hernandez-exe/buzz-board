## Why can't I buzz?

The message under your buzzer explains why:

- **Buzzer closed. Wait for the host to open it.** The host hasn't opened the buzzer for this question yet.
- **Your team already had its turn this round.** Another chance was given to everyone else. Wait for the next question.
- **Owls buzzed in first.** (with the name of whoever was faster) Another player or team got there first.
- **Buzzer open: only your captain, Ana, can buzz.** In this game only each team's captain buzzes. See [team captains](/help/teams-and-names#what-are-team-captains).
- **Pick a team to play.** Choose a team on the **Team** tab.
- **Waiting for your host to put you on a team.** In this game the host assigns teams.
- **This game has finished.** Thanks for playing!

When the buzzer is open, it turns red and says **Buzzer open: press now!**

In games that use a **queue**, the messages are a little different:

- **Buzzer open: press to join the queue!** Press to get in line. Everyone who presses lines up in order.
- **You're next in line.** or **You're 3rd in line.** Your buzz counted. Wait for your turn.
- **You're answering!** It's your turn. Answer out loud.
- **Not this time: your team sits out until the next question.** The host marked your answer wrong.
- **Buzzer closed: the host is working through the queue.** No new buzzes for now. The teams in line still get their turn.

See [Buzzing modes and undo](/help/buzzing).

## I pressed the buzzer. Did it count?

Your page shows **You buzzed in first!** or **Your team buzzed in first!** when your buzz won, or your place in line in a queue game. If someone else was faster, it shows who. A sound alone doesn't confirm anything. If an error appears under the buzzer, your press didn't count.

## Joining is closed

The host has stopped new players joining. Ask them to turn **New players can join** back on. Players already in the game can still return.

## This game has finished

Finished games can't be joined or played, and names and teams can't change. Hosts can reopen a game for a short while to correct results. See [Host a game](/help/host-quickstart#how-do-i-finish-the-game).

## The game is busy

Too many changes arrived at the same moment, and yours was not saved. Try again.

## The page says it's reconnecting

The live connection dropped. Your buzzer still works, but what you see may be out of date until it reconnects. If it says live updates stopped, reload the page.

## I was sent back to the join page

This browser no longer has your player for that game. You may have left the room on this device, cleared your browser data, or opened the game in a different browser. See [Join a game and get back in](/help/join-and-resume).
