## How do players get a team?

Hosts choose a **team assignment** setting in the host desk's **Players** section:

- **Automatic** puts each new player on the team with the fewest players. A player who comes back keeps their team.
- **Players choose** lets players pick a team on the **Team** tab after joining.
- **Host assigns** keeps new players waiting until a host moves them. The player's buzzer says it is waiting for a team.

## Can I switch teams?

When the host allows it, open the **Team** tab and choose a team. Switching isn't possible when:

- the host has turned **Players can switch teams** off,
- the host assigns teams,
- the buzzer is open (wait for it to close), or
- the game has finished.

The **Team** tab says which of these applies.

## How do hosts move players or change teams?

Use the host desk's **Players** tab: **Teams** at the top adds and deletes teams and controls switching, and the player list below renames, moves or removes players. Moves wait while the buzzer is open. Moving a player doesn't move their past points: points belong to the team that earned them. See [Managing players](/help/moderation).

## What are team captains?

By default **everyone on a team can buzz and answer**. A host can instead choose **Only team captains can buzz and answer**, either when creating a team game or later on the host desk's **Room** tab. Then each team's captain is the only one who can:

- press the buzzer, and
- save the team's answer in a [Sequential Quiz](/help/sequential-quiz).

Teammates still see everything: the buzzer messages, the team's saved answer and the chat. A teammate's buzzer says **Buzzer open: only your captain, Ana, can buzz.**

**Who is captain?** The first player to join a team. If the captain leaves the team or is removed, the next earliest member takes over, so a team with players always has one. The captain is shown with a ★ in the player's header, on the **Team** tab and on the host desk's **Players** tab.

**Changing the captain.**
- The captain can hand the role on from the **Team** tab (**Make captain** next to a teammate).
- Hosts, co-hosts and moderators can choose any member on the **Players** tab (**Make captain**).
- A change takes effect from the next buzz or answer save.

## How do I change my name or my team's name?

On the **Info** tab, select **Edit my game profile**, change your name or your team's name, then **Save changes**. This only changes how you appear in this game, not your account. Names can include emoji and letters from any language. Line breaks and hidden control characters aren't allowed, and names have a maximum length (see Rules at a glance).

Your host can lock your name or your team's name. A locked field says so; ask your host if it needs changing. If someone changes a name while you're editing it, your text is kept and you're asked to check it and save again.

Names can't be changed after the game has finished.

## How do individual games work?

In an individual game, every player scores on their own and there is no **Team** tab. Each player appears on the scoreboard under their nickname.
