Each game has a text chat, which the host turns on. Everyone in the game can use the **Everyone** channel. In team games each team also has its own channel, which only that team and the organizers can read.

## How do I turn chat on?

Chat is off in new games. On the host desk, open the **Chat** tab and turn on **Chat for this game**. Owners and co-hosts can turn it on or off at any time; turning it off keeps earlier messages but stops new ones.

## Who can see my messages?

- **Everyone** is seen by every player and organizer in the game.
- **Your team's channel** is seen by your current team and the organizers. If you move to another team, you see that team's channel instead, including its earlier messages, and you no longer see your old team's.
- Organizers (owner, co-hosts and moderators) post as **Organizer** with their role, never with their account name.

Chat is for this game only. Don't post private details such as recovery links, passwords or addresses.

## Why can't I send a message?

- **Chat is turned off for this game.** Ask your host to turn it on.
- **You're muted.** An organizer muted you, for a few minutes or until they unmute you. You can still buzz and play.
- **Only organizers can post here right now.** The channel is in staff-only mode, for example while answers are being collected.
- **Slow mode is on.** Wait a few seconds between messages.
- **You're sending messages too quickly.** Wait a moment and try again.
- **This game has finished, so chat is read-only.** You can still read it.
- **Chat is temporarily unavailable.** Your message wasn't sent. The game itself still works; try again shortly.

Messages can be up to 2,000 bytes long (about 2,000 letters, fewer with emoji or accented letters). Your text stays in the box if sending fails.

## How do I moderate chat?

Owners, co-hosts and moderators can moderate from the host desk's **Chat** tab:

- **Remove** a message. Its text is erased for everyone and can't be restored.
- **Mute** someone for 10 minutes or until you unmute them. They can still play. Moderators can mute players; owners and co-hosts can also mute moderators and co-hosts. Nobody can mute the owner.
- **Staff only** stops participants posting in a channel. **Slow mode** makes people wait between messages.
- **Pin** one message to the top of a channel, and **Clear history** to hide everything posted in a channel so far.

The **All channels** view shows the newest messages from every channel in one list, labelled by channel.

## How long are messages kept?

Message text is deleted 7 days after it was sent, and removed messages are erased straight away. Screenshots people took, and backups, can't be recalled.
